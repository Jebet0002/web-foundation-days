About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you organize your daily tasks and thoughts efficiently. It provides a simple, accessible interface for creating and managing notes on the go.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the note input field on the home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to save your note.
  3. Review your saved notes list at any time to keep track of your tasks.
